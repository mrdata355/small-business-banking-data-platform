# Public domain context

This project models generated small-business banking workflows. It does not represent Live Oak Bank's private systems, database schemas, infrastructure, controls, customer records, or internal processes.

The domain vocabulary is informed by public product and investor material so the generated workflows resemble recognizable small-business banking concerns rather than generic ecommerce examples.

## Public business areas represented

Live Oak publicly describes:

- a fully digital banking model,
- small-business lending including SBA, USDA, conventional and customized business loans,
- business checking with treasury services,
- business savings and certificates of deposit,
- specialized industry lending teams,
- continued emphasis on technology, service and efficient small-business banking.

Public references:

- https://www.liveoak.bank/

## Public specialty-industry vocabulary

The public business-loans page lists specialties such as accounting and tax, agriculture, automotive care, behavioral health, broadband, car wash, dental, education services, franchise restaurants, funeral home and cemetery, government contracting, hardware, healthcare, home care, HVAC/plumbing/electrical contractors, insurance, investment advisory, law firms, managed service providers, pet care, pharmacy, property management, assisted living, restoration/remediation/cleaning, RV parks, self-storage, senior care, technology/media/telecommunications, veterinary, and winery/vineyard.

The warehouse seed maps these public labels to project-owned analytical groups. No internal risk treatment, pricing, approval policy or segmentation is inferred from the public list.

## How the digital twin uses this context

The digital twin uses generated borrower, application, document, identity, treasury and service events across those business domains. It intentionally keeps underwriting policy, risk thresholds, SLAs, model features, costs and performance targets project-owned and synthetic.

The objective is familiarity of **business shape** while keeping every technical implementation, control and data value independent of private bank systems.
